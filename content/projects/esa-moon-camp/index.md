The ESA Moon Camp Challenge is a yearly competition for high school students to imagine how a lunar settlement could operate in the near future, and design it in Fusion 360.

Our team PIoneers won the [3rd Prize](https://www.esa.int/Education/Moon_Camp/Moon_Camp_Challenge_2020-2021_Winners).

This contest was my first encounter with CAD and a great way to learn Fusion 360.
